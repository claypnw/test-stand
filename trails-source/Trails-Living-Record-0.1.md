## 1. American Gulch Trail

Group: Trails of the Town

Inherited source link: https://discovergilacounty.com/hiking-payson-area-trails-system/

The trail stands in town, off Main Street, with trailhead posts on Webster Street and Pheasant Lane. Its two linked walks — 0.6 miles on the north side, 1 mile on the south — run in the wash under broken cliffs, past an old concrete water pipe left over from the town's early days. The wash is greener in the wet months, with big rocks to scramble, wildlife tracks in the sand, and thorny plants to step around. Easy on the north side, moderate on the south.

## 12. Water Wheel Falls

Group: Trails of the Wilds

Inherited source link: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r03/tonto/recreation/first-second-third-crossings-and-water-wheel

North of town on Houston Mesa Road, the trail runs about 1.6 miles out and back, up the East Verde River to the falls, the swimming holes, and the old rusted water wheel that gave the place its name. A federal day-use pass is required — a Tonto pass: $8 per vehicle for the day at a Forest Service office or a store that sells them, $12 at the fee machine, and the machines take debit or credit cards. The fee machines for this ground stand at Shoefly Village, Houston Mesa Campground, and the Payson Ranger Station. The Forest Service gives no months for the fee (TBD).

From the Hearth, as recorded: about a 25-minute ride from the Hearth — 10 miles by road.

## 16. Green Valley Park

Group: Parks and Grounds

Inherited source link: https://adventurepayson.com/parks/

The town's great green at the end of Main Street — 43 acres built around three lakes, stocked for fishing (a license is needed) and open to your own kayak or canoe. Around the water stand a splash pad for the children, a playground, shaded ramadas with grills for a day's feast, and an amphitheater that hosts summer concerts under the Rim. And the park holds more than play: the Rim Country Museum and the Zane Grey Cabin stand within it, so a traveler may walk from a morning's fishing straight into the country's own history. The name above leads to the town's own fuller word on the park.

From the Hearth, as recorded: less than 10 minutes from the Hearth — 7 minutes by road, 2 miles.
