Snow-globe sample

A sample only. It is not on the real album.

Hold the picture and drag it. It moves inside the globe. Left and right, up and down.

The near parts move more than the far parts. The trailer and the cart move the most. The trees and the sky move the least. That is what makes it look deep, like a real globe in your hand.

The photo was first widened past its edges. More of the same street, trees, sky, and lawn, at the same size. Then the globe was cut out of that one picture. The photo in the middle is the true photo, whole and unbent.
