Every thing in its own home — the structure audit, 2026-11-23

What is being done: every file and folder that stands under an unrelated thing's home is being moved to its own home — its own repository, its own folder, its own place — and each diagram below shows one move: where the thing stands now, and where it goes.

Why: Clay's standing rule — a thing is never stored under an unrelated thing's home, where it cannot be found. Every thing stands in its own home; sharing a home is the exception, only for closely linked things. A thing stored in the wrong place is a thing nobody can find by its name.

This page is the audit and the plan. Nothing has been moved yet. Each move, when it runs, goes in the settled order: the things that point at the old place are updated first, and the old address is turned off last. The full written audit stands with this page's record (audit.md, 2026-11-23).
