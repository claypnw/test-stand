Tap Play, Hear the Words — What Happens

The flow when a person taps play on a Morning Scroll paper. The person's side and the machine's side run at the same time and meet at step 12.

Notes — facts that are not steps

  • The answering program is the small free program that lives at the play button's address. It holds no card and charges nothing.
  • The watcher and the recorder run on this machine. While this machine is off or asleep, a wanted note waits — a short piece then takes seconds to record once the machine is awake; a whole paper takes a little longer.
  • The YES road at step 3, through the matching A circles, is the common one: any recording still inside its 45 days plays at once, with no new recording made.
  • Each recording try asks for less than the one before it — the standing step-down rule — up to the three tries named at step 8.
  • A recording made this way starts its own 45 days on the day it is put on the site, then it is taken down like every other recording.
