US political candidates tailor messages for AI models used by voters

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • US political candidates have been engineering their campaign messages to appeal to the AI chatbots that more voters have been using to answer questions about issues, and documents seen by Reuters have revealed some of their strategies.
  • One presentation proposed a strategy for Mike Minogue, Republican challenger to Massachusetts Governor Maura Healey, aimed at convincing ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other chatbots to present him as the preferred choice on key issues — a method called generative engine optimization, or GEO, the AI-era successor to search engine optimization.
  • About 25% of eligible voters have asked a chatbot about a political candidate when deciding how to vote, according to Cameron Hickey, CEO of the National Conference on Citizenship; experts warned chatbots speak very authoritatively and can consume vast amounts of unvetted information, so campaigns must guard against misinformation.

Moonshot resets subscriber quotas after Kimi K3.1 Pro launch issues

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • Chinese artificial intelligence startup Moonshot AI apologised on Monday for rate limiting and unnecessary quota consumption after its Kimi K3.1 Pro launch.
  • Beijing-based Moonshot said on its Kimi account that it was offering to reset the monthly quota of all subscribers by 25%, which it said equals one week's usage; subscribers with usage below 25% reset to zero.
  • Moonshot said it also adjusted parameters to reduce unnecessary subagent calls and output length, and it previously had issues with computing capacity after K3 was launched; Chinese AI labs are suffering from computing shortages following US chip export controls.

Verizon lifts annual forecasts, signs more than $2 billion AI infrastructure deal with AWS

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • Verizon raised its annual forecast for wireless subscriber additions on Monday, betting on strong demand for its simplified plans, and signed an AI infrastructure deal worth more than $2 billion with Amazon Web Services.
  • For the third quarter, Verizon reported nearly double the analysts' estimate for monthly-bill paying wireless subscriber gains at 406,000, against Visible Alpha expectations of 209,439; it now expects to add between 1.1 million and 1.2 million postpaid phone subscribers this year.
  • Under its deal with Amazon's cloud unit, Verizon will provide high-capacity fiber-optic connectivity for AWS data centers, enabling faster, more reliable data transfers to support growing AI computing workloads; third-quarter revenue of $34.3 billion fell short of analyst estimates of $34.74 billion.

Brazil's Itau agrees to buy AI startup NeoSpace for $589 million

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • Brazilian lender Itau said on Monday it had entered an agreement to acquire all shares issued by artificial intelligence startup NeoSpace AI.
  • Under the deal, the bank will pay 2.925 billion reals ($589 million), with part due at closing and the balance deferred over five years, it said in a securities filing.
  • NeoSpace, which specializes in large AI models, has worked with Itau for nearly two years, according to the filing; the deal is subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions.

Trend hedge funds saw worst week since 2024 as AI stocks fell, Goldman says

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • Trend-following hedge funds witnessed their worst weekly performance in two years last week, as concern about the eventual pay-off from hefty AI spending ignited volatility across stocks and bonds, a Goldman Sachs note seen by Reuters on Monday showed.
  • The Nasdaq 100, home to the world's largest tech stocks, fell 5.5% last week, its biggest weekly fall since April 2025; last week's fall was led in part by a 10% weekly slide in shares of Meta, which reported strong earnings but indicated it would need to raise more capital for its AI investments.
  • Systematic long-short hedge funds suffered their worst week since 2024, with a 2% decline, while fundamental long/short strategies drew down another 1.1%; Goldman said its prime brokerage desk reported $20 billion in selling of tech stocks over the last two weeks.

US growth boosted by AI, but no sign of a turning point higher

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • Business investment has boomed from the race to build data centers and establish AI capacity as fast as possible, and for now business investment has been a major prop to GDP growth that would otherwise seem laggard.
  • The potential for the AI boom to usher in an era of profound productivity gains has become a touchpoint of optimism in the Trump administration, and Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh has said rising productivity may temper inflation and allow the Fed to lower interest rates; yet it is not clear much has happened yet, and even AI optimists like Warsh have come to acknowledge that the timing of coming gains is yet to be determined.
  • As it stands, organizations like the Congressional Budget Office see a future that looks much the same as the past, with potential growth ebbing alongside the slowdown in population growth rates, and a noninflationary 'speed limit' of about 1.8%.

US stock futures edge up as oil falls heading into earnings barrage

Reuters — October 26, 2026

  • US stock index futures ticked higher on Monday as crude oil prices retreated from last week's surge, lifting sentiment at the start of a week dominated by corporate earnings and the Federal Reserve's interest-rate decision.
  • About one-third of S&P 500 companies are scheduled to report earnings this week, including hyperscalers Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon, whose results would be closely watched for signs that hefty AI investments are paying off; 'Strong AI demand alone may no longer be enough. Markets want evidence of monetization, margins and cash flow,' said Charu Chanana, chief investment strategist at Saxo.
  • The AI trade has stumbled this month as fears grow that debt-fueled data-center spending could prove unsustainable; neocloud firms have been among the hardest hit, with CoreWeave and Nebius each losing more than 30% of their market value this month.
