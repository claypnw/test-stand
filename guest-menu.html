Mogollon’s Hearth Guest Book

Mogollon’s Hearth Guest Book is an app on this tablet. It stands on the tablet’s home screen.

In it, you take a picture as you wander, and then you tell that picture — in your own voice, or in your own written words. Each picture and its tale are kept together in the app.

Nothing in the app is sent anywhere. It stays here, for the making of your visit’s story.

The Hearth’s Story

Mogollon’s Hearth is a guest house in Payson, Arizona. Its host built it as a real-life hearth hall in the Skyrim way — full size, raised by hand, not a model.

Guests rest here by the fire, walk the trails, and ride out to see the holds of the country round about.

Welcome to the Hearth. The fire is lit.

Ask your host if anything here does not open.
